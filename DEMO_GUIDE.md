# NEURONEXUS — Hackathon Judge Demonstration Guide

**Project:** NEURONEXUS  
**Team:** Infinite Loopers (Sathyabama Institute of Science and Technology)  
**Primary Objective:** Deliver a reliable, polished end-to-end demonstration that judges can understand within two minutes and explore within five minutes.

---

## ⚡ The 2-Minute Speed Run (Fast Demo)

### Step 1: Open the Application (0:00 - 0:15)
- Double-click `run.bat` or open `index.html` in Chrome/Edge/Firefox.
- Point out the Hero Section: **"Your Learning Has a Pattern. Discover It."**
- Note the team badge: *Infinite Loopers • Sathyabama Hackathon Edition*.
- Highlight that the platform is running live with zero server installation blockers.

### Step 2: Launch the NeuroLearn Loop (0:15 - 0:45)
- Click **"Start NeuroLearn Loop"** on the hero banner.
- Select the recommended topic: **Trees & Binary Search Trees** (CSE-301).
- Click **"Launch Assessment Quiz (10 Qs)"**.
- In the quiz view, point out the timed assessment and granular subtopics (*BST Operations*, *Tree Traversal*, *Tree Balancing*).
- Click the bottom helper: **"Auto-fill 60% (Baseline with Gaps)"** and hit **Submit Assessment**.

### Step 3: Discover Gaps & Explainable Revision (0:45 - 1:15)
- Show **Stage 2 (Discover):**
  - Point out that BST Operations is 100%, but **Tree Traversal is 25%** and **Tree Balancing is 66.7%**.
  - Show the identified gaps with explicit mathematical reasons.
- Show **Stage 3 (Personalize):**
  - Highlight the generated Revision Roadmap.
  - Read one rationale aloud: *"Recommended because your accuracy in Tree Traversal was 25%, significantly lower than other evaluated subtopics."*

### Step 4: Measure Real Improvement (1:15 - 1:45)
- Click **"Execute Step →"** on the Follow-up Assessment.
- In the retake quiz, click **"Auto-fill 90% (Mastery / Retake)"** and submit.
- Show **Stage 4 (Improve):**
  - Point out the **Net Delta banner: +30.0%**.
  - Show the side-by-side comparison chart: Baseline 60% → Retake 90%.
  - Point out that Tree Traversal jumped from 25% to 100%!

### Step 5: Wrap Up with Ethics & Privacy (1:45 - 2:00)
- Click **"Privacy"** in the top navigation.
- Highlight the **Voluntary Opt-In** and **Transparent Rejection Policy** (<70% coverage withholds estimates).
- Conclude: *"NEURONEXUS turns assessment scores into measurable, explainable learning improvements."*
