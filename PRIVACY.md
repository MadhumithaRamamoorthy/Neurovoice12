# NEURONEXUS — Privacy, Consent & Ethical Neurotechnology Policy

## 1. Core Ethical Principles
NEURONEXUS adheres to strict ethical guidelines regarding biometrics, artificial intelligence, and educational analytics.

### Principle 1: Academic Sovereign Autonomy
- Using NEURONEXUS requires zero biometric sensors. All educational modules—including courses, lessons, quizzes, community Q&A, and AI tutoring—operate fully without EEG hardware.
- Connecting an EEG device is 100% voluntary and opt-in.

### Principle 2: Strict Purpose Limitation & Non-Punitive Guarantee
- **Never Used for Grading:** EEG contextual estimates are strictly informative for the individual student and can **never** be used for grading, exam proctoring, or academic punishment.
- **Faculty Shield:** Individual biometric traces are strictly shielded from teachers and parents. Instructors only see aggregated, anonymized academic quiz error patterns to improve syllabus delivery.
- **No Ranking:** Students are never ranked by focus scores.

### Principle 3: Non-Diagnostic Boundaries
- NEURONEXUS makes **no claim of medical, clinical, or neurological diagnosis**.
- Inferred focus indicators are statistical approximations derived from single-channel frontal biopotentials. High focus does not prove comprehension, nor does low focus indicate cognitive deficiency.

### Principle 4: Transparent Signal Rejection (Anti-Fabrication)
- When sensor contact impedance is high or valid signal coverage falls below the 70% threshold, NEURONEXUS **transparently withholds the estimate**.
- The platform explicitly displays:
  > *"Focus estimate unavailable because the session does not contain enough valid signal data."*
- We never generate random or fabricated estimates to make dashboards look impressive.

### Principle 5: Sovereign Data Ownership & Instant Wipe
- Students can download their entire assessment and session history as an open JSON file at any time.
- Students can wipe all stored local records instantly with a single click.
